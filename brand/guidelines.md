# Brand guidelines (working notes)

Rules of thumb for designing PauseAI en Español merchandise. When in doubt,
defer to the canonical [PauseAI brand guide](https://pauseai.info) upstream
— this file documents the conventions we extend from.

## Voice

- **Earnest, hopeful, serious.** Not snarky, not doomy, not edgy.
- The wearer of a PauseAI shirt should look like someone you'd want to talk
  to about AI policy, not someone who's just yelling.
- Spanish copy should be neutral Spanish (España + Latin America readable).
  Avoid heavy regionalisms unless the design is explicitly local.

## Color use

- The orange `#FF9416` is the brand. Use it as the dominant accent or as a
  full-fill brand-forward color. Avoid combining it with other vibrant hues.
- Pair orange with ink (`#111`) or paper (`#FFF`) — these two are the only
  "supporting" colors needed for most designs.
- Gradients from `#FF9416` → `#FF6600` or `#FF9416` → `#C45A00` are
  acceptable for digital display but **avoid on apparel** (gradients cost
  more, band in screen-print, and look muddy on DTG).

## Logo use

- The PauseAI ES logo lives at `brand/logos/pauseai-es-on-light.svg`. Do not redraw,
  recolor, re-letter, or distort it. If a design needs an alternative
  treatment, raise an issue first.
- **Clear space:** the height of the logo's capital "P" on all four sides.
- **Minimum size:** 20 mm wide on physical print. Below that the wordmark
  becomes unreadable.

## Typography

- Headings, wordmarks, slogans: **Saira Condensed**.
- Body, taglines, long-form copy: **Roboto Slab**.
- See `brand/fonts.md` for licensing and embedding rules.
- Don't mix in additional typefaces. Two is enough.

## Composition

- One strong idea per piece. Designs should feel intentional, not crowded.
- Center of mass should sit visually *above* the geometric center of the
  print area — what looks centered on a flat layout sits low on a chest.
- For T-shirts: avoid touching the print-area boundary; leave a 5 mm safety
  margin. See `products/tshirt.yaml`.
- For stickers: 3 mm bleed, 3 mm safe area, design to the *visible* edge
  but cut tolerance is real.

## Anti-patterns

- Multiple competing typefaces (stick to the two brand fonts).
- Photorealistic AI-generated imagery — it muddles our message about AI.
- Mocking individuals or organizations.
- Mushroom clouds, bombs, or other catastrophe imagery — we argue for
  caution, not catastrophe-porn.
- Edgy or partisan copy.
- Decorative gradients that don't print well.
