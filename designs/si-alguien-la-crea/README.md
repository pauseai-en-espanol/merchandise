# si-alguien-la-crea

> The verbatim title of the Spanish edition of the 2025 book *If Anyone
> Builds It, Everyone Dies* — **«Si alguien la crea, todos moriremos»** —
> set in the chapter's cais-extincion idiom: packed display type with the
> consequence (`TODOS MORIREMOS`) big in the display accent colour and the
> setup (`si alguien la crea`) small in ink. Two PauseAI editorial asides
> are woven in as small ink italic — a gloss on what *"la / it"* is, and an
> honest hedge on the verb. From across a room it reads as the book's stark
> claim; up close, the asides reveal PauseAI's more precise, more honest
> version.

### The two asides are PauseAI's, not the book's

To keep the verbatim rule honest, the parenthetical asides are visually
distinct (small ink italic, parenthesised) and are **PauseAI
annotation**, not part of the quoted title:

- **`(la superinteligencia artificial)`** — glosses the pronoun
  *"la" / "it"*, **verbatim from the book's own subtitle**, so the gloss
  is sourced from the book and stays on-message (PauseAI is about frontier
  / superintelligent AI, not all advanced AI).
- **`(muy probablemente)`** — PauseAI's honest hedge on the book's
  absolute *"moriremos"*. Deliberately echoes Sam Altman's verbatim *"muy
  probablemente"* in `designs/altman-fin-del-mundo/` (his 2015 "…most
  likely … the end of the world").
