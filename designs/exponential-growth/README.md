# exponential-growth

**ES:** Crecimiento exponencial · *formerly `crecimiento-exponencial`*

> **`×5` CADA AÑO**: how fast the compute used to train frontier language
> models has grown since 2020, per Epoch AI. Below it, notable models from
> AlexNet (2012) to GPT-6 Astra (2026) are dots on a **linear** scale, with a
> smooth exponential trend through them: flat for a decade, then a wall. No
> adjectives and no scare words. The curve does the talking, and the question
> it leaves ("¿y el año que viene?") is the conversation.

**Deliberately left out:**
- **Claude Opus / Mythos, GPT-5.1 → 5.6:** Epoch has **no** training-compute
  estimate for any of them in any of its datasets (Notable, All, Large-scale,
  Frontier; checked 2026-09-28). The most it says is a note on Opus 4 / 4.5
  and GPT-5.1: "very likely >1e25 FLOP", a floor rather than an estimate.
  On a linear axis that floor would sit on the baseline and understate them
  badly. They go in when Epoch publishes estimates.
- **GPT-5** (2025-08-07, 6.6e25, Speculative, CI 2e25–2e26): real Epoch data,
  but it sits far *below* GPT-4.5 (Epoch estimates less pre-training compute
  for GPT-5). On a shirt, that reads as an error and needs a footnote.
- **Grok 3** (3.5e26): lands almost on top of GPT-4.5. Where dots overlap we
  keep the most relevant one.
- **Claude 3.5 / 3.7 Sonnet:** they have estimates, but they aren't Anthropic's
  frontier models.

### The trend curve

1. **Growth rate:** least squares on log10(FLOP) vs year over the plotted
   points gives **×5.07 per year**, printed as "×5,1 al año, ajuste a estos
   modelos". It agrees with Epoch's ×5 (CI 4–6×).
2. **Height:** with that rate fixed, we pick the scale factor that best fits
   the points' actual values on the linear axis. This makes the curve follow
   what the eye sees (the recent frontier) instead of being dominated by the
   near-zero early models.

The time axis is **broken** between 2013.6 and 2018 (marked `//`). Values are
~0 there anyway, and the break gives the crowded recent years room.

### Does the tail "fit"?

GPT-6 Astra is ~2× Grok 4 over 14 months, slower than ×5/yr would predict.
But it is a single fresh estimate (Epoch's CI ≈ 5e26–2e27), and much of the
recent frontier has no public figure. The chart doesn't claim the trend
continues or stops, and the headline is Epoch's own figure. **Re-check the
CSV before every print run**, then update `DATA` / `RETRIEVED` and re-run
the script.

### Earlier versions (rejected)

- A log scale: correct, but it reads as a gentle line to non-specialists.
- Dots joined by straight segments: looked "spiky", and "≈ 3.000 × GPT-3"
  lacked context.
- Illustrative ×5 bars: clear, but they lost the real models.
- A "sin dato público" strip of release-date ticks for Opus/Mythos/GPT-5.x:
  read as noise.
