# cais-extincion

> The 2023 Center for AI Safety "Statement on AI Risk" — signed by the
> CEOs of every frontier AI lab — quoted verbatim, with the
> signatories listed underneath. The implicit accusation is the
> contradiction between what they signed in May 2023 and what they're
> still building today.

## Voice lane

**B — Accountability / protest.** The accountability ratio is the
highest in the chapter set: the statement is one sentence, the
signatories are a matter of public record, and the contradiction
between what they signed and what they're building writes itself.

## Sources (verified)

- **Statement (English original):** "Mitigating the risk of extinction
  from AI should be a global priority alongside other societal-scale
  risks such as pandemics and nuclear war."
- **Authority:** Center for AI Safety (CAIS), https://www.safe.ai
- **Date:** **30 May 2023**
- **URL:** https://www.safe.ai/work/statement-on-ai-risk
- **Signatories named on the design (all on the public CAIS list):**
  - **Sam Altman** — CEO, OpenAI
  - **Demis Hassabis** — CEO, Google DeepMind
  - **Dario Amodei** — CEO, Anthropic
  - **Geoffrey Hinton** — ex-Google, 2024 Nobel laureate
  - **Yoshua Bengio** — Mila, Université de Montréal
  - **Ilya Sutskever** — ex-OpenAI co-founder; in 2024 founded
    Safe Superintelligence Inc. (another frontier lab)
  - **Bill Gates** — Microsoft co-founder; major AI investor
  - **Stuart Russell** — Berkeley; co-author of the standard AI
    textbook
  - (Plus hundreds more — see safe.ai for the full list. Elon
    Musk is sometimes confused for a signer; he did **not** sign
    this statement. He signed the separate March 2023 FLI "Pause
    Giant AI Experiments" letter.)

- **Spanish translation on the design:** «Mitigar el riesgo de
  extinción por la IA debería ser una prioridad global...» — opening
  fragment of the full English statement; the ellipsis marks the
  truncation so the tee reads as a quoted excerpt, not a paraphrase.
  The omitted tail ("alongside other societal-scale risks such as
  pandemics and nuclear war") is dropped only for fit / impact; the
  full text is in the source field above for anyone who asks.
