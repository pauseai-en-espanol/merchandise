# altman-fin-del-mundo

> Three-line build of Sam Altman's verbatim "fin del mundo" quote —
> `MUY PROBABLEMENTE` → `LA IA CONDUCIRÁ AL` → `FIN DEL MUNDO` — with
> `IA` highlighted in white inside the middle line. Below: a small
> hand-drawn portrait stencil + compact attribution.
>
> Originally based on a PSD draft by a chapter contributor.

  there will be great companies created with serious machine
  learning."
- **Author:** Sam Altman, then president of Y Combinator; CEO of
  OpenAI from 2019.
- **Date / venue:** **June 2015**, at a tech conference. Cited in
  Stanford SIEPR, *"At what point do we decide AI's risks outweigh

  https://siepr.stanford.edu/news/what-point-do-we-decide-ais-risks-outweigh-its-promise
- **Spanish rendering on the design:** `«MUY PROBABLEMENTE / LA IA
  CONDUCIRÁ AL / FIN DEL MUNDO.»` — faithful condensation of the
  English original; the "I think that" and "sort of" hedges are
  dropped for chest-print legibility, guillemets and the ellipsis

- **Portrait stencil:** original hand-drawn line illustration in the
  contributor PSD, vectorized via potrace.

## How this design is assembled (atypical)

This design **deviates** from the chapter's normal authoring rules:

1. **Embedded raster text.** Unlike other chapter designs, the three
   quote lines + the attribution are not `<text>` elements. They are
   PNGs (the PSD layers' cached bitmaps, upscaled 8× to **328 DPI at
   24 cm print**) embedded as base64 `<image>` blocks.
   - Why: the contributor's typography (Bebas Neue Regular per the
     PSD metadata) doesn't render identically without the font, and
     vectorizing the bitmap produced artifacts because the letters'
     anti-aliased edges chain together as one connected component.
   - Consequence: the SVG is ~540 KB instead of ~20 KB; the text is
     not searchable or restylable.
2. **Non-brand font visible in the design.** Bebas Neue Regular is
   not on the chapter's brand spec (Saira Condensed / Montserrat /
   Roboto Slab — see `brand/fonts.md`). The contributor's PSD uses
   it; we honor that. If/when this design is approved for print,
   this exception should be reviewed.
3. **Header (chapter logo) is vector**, sized 145 × 40 mm and not
   subject to the design's black↔white↔orange color swap rule.

|---|---|---|

328 DPI is well above the 150-DPI DTG minimum and the 200-DPI screen-
print recommendation.

## Deliberate changes from the contributor PSD

- **Logo size: 145 × 40 mm (~60 % of canvas width)** vs. the PSD's
  ~50 × 15 mm (~11 %). The PSD logo was a placeholder.
- The PSD's "Orange Cover" darker-orange backdrop field (`#FF7F02`)
  is **dropped** — it adds an off-brand orange shade.
- **Guillemets `«…»` added** around the quote (open on line 1, close
  on line 3) for chapter typographic consistency.
- **Stencil background transparent** — the PSD's face circle had a
  white scan-card behind the line drawing; here the drawing reads
  directly on the tee. Stencil itself is vector (potrace), so the
  background is naturally transparent.
- **Date corrected: `c.2014` → `2015`** — the PSD draft attribution
  read `Sam altman, c.2014`; chapter-verified source places it at
  June 2015. Attribution re-rendered with BebasNeue-Regular.
