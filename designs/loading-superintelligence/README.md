# loading-superintelligence

**ES:** Cargando superinteligencia · *formerly `cargando-superinteligencia`*

> **«Cargando superinteligencia…»** A retro block progress bar stalled at
> 90 %, "Tiempo restante: nadie lo sabe", and underneath it the pause
> symbol, big and white, the same glyph as the PauseAI mark. Payoff:
> **PAUSAR `NO ES` PARAR.** The joke lands first (everyone has stared at a
> stuck progress bar), then the argument: we are close to something whose
> arrival time nobody can predict, and the ask is a *pause* until it's
> safe, not an end to AI.

## Voice lane

**A — Thoughtful policy conversation.** A smile first, the argument
second. It also answers the most common objection ("so you want to stop
AI?") on the shirt itself.

## Status

`draft`: ES + EN on orange, white and black tees. The white/black variants
are generated by `scripts/build-loading-superintelligence.py` (standard rule, `scripts/tee_variants.py`).
