# regulado-como-un-sandwich

> A sandwich drawn in layers, each layer labelled with a **real EU food
> regulation** (hygiene, allergens and labelling, traceability, official
> controls). Under it, Stuart Russell's line: **«Incluso los sándwiches
> están `MUCHO MÁS REGULADOS` que los sistemas de IA.»** The visual is the
> proof: this is what "regulated" looks like for a lunch. The argument is
> that regulating something risky isn't exotic or anti-innovation. We
> already do it for sandwiches.

## Voice lane

**B — Accountability / protest.** A verified public quote, plus regulations

## Sources (verified)

### The quote

> "Even sandwiches are much more regulated than AI systems."

- **Who:** Stuart Russell, Professor of Computer Science, UC Berkeley.
- **Where / when:** on stage at the **CogX Festival, London**, on Tuesday
  12 September 2023 (the print says only "sept. 2023").
- **Reported by:** Chloe Taylor, "AI is coming, and experts can't agree how
  to control it", *Fortune*, **14 September 2023**:
  https://fortune.com/2023/09/14/ai-regulation-mi6-law-end-of-world-elon-musk-steve-wozniak-reid-hoffman-big-tech-stuart-russell-rules-artificial-intelligence-cogx-festival/
  - Context, verbatim from the article: *"Would you like to get on a plane
    that no one has ever tested to make sure it's been tested?" he asked the
    audience on Tuesday. "Even sandwiches are much more regulated than AI
    systems."*
  - Note: the article's standfirst paraphrases this as "Even sandwiches are
    more regulated than artificial intelligence". We print the **quoted
    sentence from the body**, not the paraphrase.
- **Spanish rendering:** «Incluso los sándwiches están mucho más regulados
  que los sistemas de IA.» This is our translation. The English original is
  kept here, not on the print.
- **Don't confuse with:** Max Tegmark / FLI's "AI is also less regulated than
  sandwiches" (AI Safety Index, Dec 2025). That's a different speaker and
  wording, and it is not used here.

### The regulations on the sandwich

All in force in Spain as EU law:

| Layer label | Regulation |
|---|---|
| Higiene | Regulation (EC) No **852/2004** on the hygiene of foodstuffs |
| Alérgenos y etiquetado | Regulation (EU) No **1169/2011** on food information to consumers (allergen declaration, labelling) |
| Trazabilidad | Regulation (EC) No **178/2002**, the General Food Law (traceability, art. 18) |
| Controles oficiales | Regulation (EU) **2017/625** on official controls |

### Why there is no "AI checklist"

An earlier idea put empty checkboxes next to "frontier AI" ("mandatory safety
evaluation ☐", "licence ☐"). We dropped it. In the EU, the **AI Act** has
applied to general-purpose AI models since **2 August 2025**, and the
largest ("systemic-risk") models must do evaluations, adversarial testing and
incident reporting. An empty checklist would be misleading on a Spanish
chapter's tee. The quote is dated and attributed, so it stands on its own.
