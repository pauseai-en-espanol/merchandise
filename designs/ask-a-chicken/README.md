# ask-a-chicken

**ES:** Pregunta a un pollo · *formerly `pregunta-a-un-pollo`*

> A bold linocut chicken, drawn in the same style as the shoggoth. Beside it is
> Geoffrey Hinton's line: **"If you want to know what life's like when
> you're not the apex intelligence, `ask a chicken.`"** It's funny, instantly
> understood, and hard to forget. It comes from the Nobel-winning "godfather
> of AI", which means a stranger can't wave it away as activist hyperbole.

## Voice lane

**B — Accountability / protest**, at the warm end. It quotes a public
figure verbatim, with the joke doing the heavy lifting. The hen isn't
mocked; she's the expert witness.

## Source (verified 2026-09-28)

- **Quote (verbatim):** *"If you want to know what life's like when you're
  not the apex intelligence, ask a chicken."*
- **Who:** Geoffrey Hinton, 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics (shared with John
  Hopfield) and 2018 Turing Award.
- **Where / when:** *The Diary of a CEO* with Steven Bartlett, published
  **16 June 2025**. YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=giT0ytynSqg
  (current title: "Godfather of AI: They Keep Silencing Me But I'm Trying to
  Warn Them!").
  - **At 29:55** in the main conversation, the sentence as printed. We
    checked it against the video's own English captions.
  - It also appears in the cold open (0:49) as "…And if you want to know…",
    preceded by "And". We print the standalone 29:55 version.
  - Independent transcript with the same wording: The Singju Post, 18 June
    2025,
    https://singjupost.com/transcript-of-godfather-of-ai-i-tried-to-warn-them-but-weve-already-lost-control/
- **Variants in circulation:** secondhand paraphrases such as "…what it's
  like not to be the apex intelligence…" are not what he said here. Don't
  "correct" the print to them.
- **Spanish rendering:** «Si quieres saber cómo es la vida cuando no eres la
  inteligencia dominante, pregúntale a un pollo.» is our translation.
  "Apex intelligence" → "inteligencia dominante", and "chicken" → "pollo"
  (the natural spoken word; "gallina" was the literal alternative).

## How the art is made

`scripts/draw-chicken.py` draws the hen with the helpers of
`scripts/draw-shoggoth.py` (splines, blobs, carved knock-outs, the same eye
function), traces each colour with potrace, and sets the justified text
column from Saira Condensed's real advance widths. Edit it there and re-run
(venv with pillow, numpy, scipy, fonttools + potrace).
